# Design decision

Use this template for a consequential design choice: one where a competent engineer
could reasonably choose a different path, or where the wrong choice would mislead a
learner, reviewer, or production operator.

## Problem

What are we trying to make possible, and what failure would otherwise pass unnoticed?

## Decision

What did we choose?

## Instead of

What credible alternative did we reject?

## Why

What consequence made the selected choice better for this scope?

## Official guidance considered

Link only to sources that directly influenced the decision. Include the source title,
publisher, and access date when the guidance can change.

## Implementation

Link to the code, configuration, or workflow that carries this decision.

## Verification

What test, negative control, runnable example, or review checks the decision?

## Costs if wrong

What becomes unsafe, expensive, confusing, or harder to change if this decision is
wrong?

## What this does not prove

State the claim boundary. Do not turn a synthetic result, a documentation link, or a
successful demo into a claim about all models, users, deployments, or organizations.

## Decided by

Name the person or review authority and the date.

## Teaching link

How should a newer engineer use this record to understand the design or try it safely?
